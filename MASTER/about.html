Our Mandate

Mission and Vision

The global mandate of the Redeemed Christian Church of God that drives our parish in Abuja.

Our Vision

To make heaven.
To take as many people with us.
To have a member of RCCG in every family of all nations.

Our Mission

To accomplish our vision, holiness will be our lifestyle.
We will plant churches within five minutes walking distance in every city and town of developing countries and within five minutes driving distance in every city and town of developed countries.

What We Believe

Our Beliefs

Rooted firmly in the scriptures and the undiluted teaching of Jesus Christ.

The Bible

We believe the entire Bible is the inspired Word of God and the infallible rule of faith and conduct.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, who died for our sins, resurrected, and is the only Savior of the world.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, empowering believers for life, service, and the demonstration of God's power.

Water Baptism

We believe in baptism by immersion in water as an outward sign of an inward grace and repentance.

Holiness

We believe in a life of holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord.

The Second Coming

We await the imminent return of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, to gather His church.

A Rich Legacy

Our History & Structure

The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) was founded in 1952 by Rev. Josiah Olufemi Akindayomi. Upon his passing, Pastor Enoch Adejare Adeboye took over as the General Overseer in 1981.

Since then, the church has experienced phenomenal growth, spreading to over 198 nations of the world. Our administrative structure emphasizes accountability, grassroot evangelism, and structured discipleship through provinces, zones, areas, and local parishes.

Christ the Anchor Parish operates effectively within FCT Province 17, advancing this divine mandate across Abuja through dynamic worship, community outreach, and fervent prayer.
